Scripture editions used by BibleAI

The King James Version (standardized 1769 text) and the World English Bible
come from the open-bibles data repository used by bible-api.com.
https://github.com/seven1m/bible_api
https://github.com/seven1m/open-bibles/tree/8b330654ade69f7c96268c761da18dc83a1f49b7

King James Version. Courtesy of the CrossWire Bible Society and eBible.org.
The text is public domain outside the United Kingdom. The source identifies
UK Crown rights under letters patent. Cambridge University Press administers
permissions for the Authorized Version. UK commercial app permission has
not been established by the source repository's public domain label.
https://ebible.org/eng-kjv/copyright.htm
https://www.cambridge.org/gb/rights-and-permissions

World English Bible. Public domain. The name “World English Bible” is a
trademark of eBible.org and is reserved for faithful copies of the translation.
https://ebible.org/engwebp/copyright.htm

The bundled edition covers the reader's 66-book canon. It retains the
provider's verse numbers and text, normalizing only display whitespace.
Headings, notes and five WEB verse numbers containing only footnotes are
excluded, following the provider's text-only representation.

Rebuild with bible_parser 1.1.4 (the provider's pinned parser), Ruby sqlite3
with FTS5, and the two XML files from the repository revision above.
Pass the directory containing eng-kjv.osis.xml and eng-web.usfx.xml to
ruby scripts/build-scripture-index.rb. The builder checks both SHA-256 hashes.

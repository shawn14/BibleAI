Make room for Scripture, one verse at a time.

BibleAI is a quiet place to read the King James Version, keep meaningful passages, and build a daily reading habit. Open the Bible, swipe to the next verse, and return to your saved place when you come back.

READ AT YOUR OWN PACE
Give each verse your attention. Swipe through a chapter or find a passage by reference, such as John 3:16. Adjust the text size and choose serif or system type for a comfortable reading experience.

KEEP WHAT SPEAKS TO YOU
Save a verse with a tap. Add a personal note, return to it in Saved, and share Scripture with someone you care about.

FOLLOW A CLEAR NEXT STEP
Choose from four guided plans: Gospel Tour, Psalms in a Month, New Testament in 30 Days, and Proverbs Wisdom. Mark a reading complete, see your progress, and keep going when you are ready.

BUILD A DAILY HABIT
Choose a reading reminder and see your streak as you complete plan readings. Your next session can be a few quiet minutes with Scripture.

EXPLORE A PASSAGE
Verse-study options include a simple explanation, historical context, related Scripture, and language notes. Study requires a compatible device with iOS 26 or iPadOS 26, Apple Intelligence enabled, and its on-device model available. AI commentary can make mistakes; use it as a reading aid and check it against the passage.

READING AND PREMIUM
Bible reading, saved verses, notes, reading plans, and streaks do not require a subscription. On supported devices, the free study allowance is five studies per day; Premium offers unlimited studies. Monthly and yearly plans are available. Any introductory trial is shown with its eligibility, duration, renewal price, and terms before purchase.

Payment is charged to your Apple ID. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple account settings. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

An internet connection is needed to load chapters you have not opened before. Previously opened chapters are cached on your device.

What if scrolling actually fed your soul?

BibleAI turns the habit you already have into time in God's Word. Instead of an endless feed, you swipe through the Bible one verse at a time, and the app remembers exactly where you left off.

ONE VERSE AT A TIME
Each verse fills the screen in a clean, quiet layout. Swipe up for the next one and keep going straight into the next chapter. Tap the book and chapter to jump anywhere in all 66 books of the King James Version.

EVERY VERSE HAS A STORY
Tap any verse to open a study. Choose Explain This Verse, Explain It Simply, Historical Context, Related Scripture, or Original Language for key Greek and Hebrew words. Studies run privately on your iPhone with Apple Intelligence.

READ THE WHOLE BIBLE, ONE DAY AT A TIME
Follow a guided reading plan: a Gospel Tour, Psalms in a Month, the New Testament in 30 Days, or a month of Proverbs. Check off each day and watch your progress grow.

KEEP WHAT SPEAKS TO YOU
Heart a verse to save it, add a personal note, and share Scripture with friends and family. Your saved verses and notes stay on your device.

SIMPLE BY DESIGN
No ads, no feed, no noise. Just Scripture, with a serif reading font, adjustable text size, and light and dark mode.

BIBLEAI PREMIUM
Reading, saving, notes and reading plans are free. Free users get 5 verse studies a day. Premium unlocks unlimited studies. Choose a monthly or yearly subscription. Payment is charged to your Apple ID and renews automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel anytime in Settings.

Verse study requires Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or newer with iOS 26). AI study is a reading aid; read it alongside the chapter.

No app account or sign-in is required. Reading, saved verses, notes and plans do not require a subscription. Close the subscription screen to continue reading. Premium is optional.

To test the core flow, finish onboarding, open The Bible, swipe between verses, tap the heart to save, and use the note control to add a reflection. Saved contains the verse and note. Use search for John 3:16. Plans contains four guided selections; mark a reading complete to update progress and streak.

Verse study uses Apple's Foundation Models framework. It requires compatible iPhone or iPad hardware, iOS 26 or iPadOS 26, Apple Intelligence enabled, and the on-device model available. Unsupported devices show an availability message; reading, saving, notes and plans remain accessible. AI commentary may be inaccurate, and every study has a report-problem action.

The study allowance is five per day. Monthly or yearly Premium unlocks unlimited study on compatible devices. StoreKit/RevenueCat determines introductory eligibility and the app displays the actual duration, renewal price and terms before purchase. Restore is available on the subscription screen. No universal trial eligibility is promised.

The King James Version is requested one chapter at a time from bible-api.com. Previously opened chapters are cached; first-time chapter downloads require internet. No account credentials are needed for review.

Onboarding offers goal, familiarity, reading-time and reminder choices. Daily reminders can be changed or disabled in Settings > Daily Reading. iCloud key-value sync uses the user's existing Apple account; no separate Bright Scroll account is created.

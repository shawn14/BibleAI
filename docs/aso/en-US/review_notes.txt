Sign-in is optional: the Account tab offers Sign in with Apple, Sign Out and Delete Account (Account > Delete Account removes the stored Apple sign-in; BibleAI has no server account). Reading needs no sign-in. Reading, saved verses, notes and plans do not require a subscription. Close the subscription screen to continue reading. Premium is optional.

To test the core flow, finish onboarding, open The Bible, swipe between verses, tap the heart to save, and use the note control to add a reflection. Saved contains the verse and note. Use search for John 3:16. Choose Five Minutes in the Gospels in Plans, open its short passage, then tap Finish today’s reading. The Bible tab shows the next day and the streak. Search “be still,” open Psalm 46:10, and use Return to my reading.

Prepared Gospel Tour guides and their related passages work offline on supported iOS 17+ devices without Apple Intelligence. Optional generated verse study uses Apple’s Foundation Models framework. It requires compatible iPhone or iPad hardware, iOS 26 or iPadOS 26, Apple Intelligence enabled, and the on-device model available. Unsupported devices show an availability message; reading, saving, notes and plans remain accessible. AI commentary may be inaccurate, and every study has a report-problem action.

The study allowance is five per day. Monthly or yearly Premium unlocks unlimited study on compatible devices. StoreKit/RevenueCat determines introductory eligibility and the app displays the actual duration, renewal price and terms before purchase. Restore is available on the subscription screen. No universal trial eligibility is promised.

The app includes the complete King James Version and World English Bible in a searchable offline Scripture index. The United Kingdom is excluded from App Store distribution. No account credentials are needed for review.

Onboarding offers goal, familiarity, reading-time and reminder choices. Daily reminders can be changed or disabled in Settings > Daily Reading. iCloud key-value sync uses the user's existing Apple account; no separate BibleAI account is created.
